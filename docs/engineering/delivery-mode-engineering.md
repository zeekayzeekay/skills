## What it does

Calibrates development and review to the project's current operating promise. Required core results stay correct and evidenced; operational rigor follows the actual users, environment and exposure. A possible future product can justify an inexpensive boundary without activating its future infrastructure.

The skill is bundled in this fork and remains independently usable. One delivery contract carries the current bar across planning, implementation and review.

## When to reach for it

Type `/delivery-mode-engineering`, or let the agent reach for it during substantial project or module work. The surrounding skills use its relevant rules within their existing task.

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Starting a retained prototype or personal tool | Declare the proof, reachable floor and current readiness evidence |
| Preparing specifications, tickets or a review | Carry the same current acceptance bar into the next artifact or reviewer |
| A future team or product is influencing today's architecture | Apply the OPTION test to small boundaries that avoid expensive reversal |
| Users, exposure or operating promises are changing | Assess the resulting gaps and any requested promotion |
| A routine fix under an existing contract | Inherit the contract and apply only the relevant rules |

## Proof, floor and seams

The **proof** is the useful result the current slice must establish. The **floor** protects outcomes reachable now, such as truthful accounting, intended effects and declared privacy boundaries. A **seam** preserves a credible future option when a small boundary avoids concrete redesign cost. These are different reasons to do work; future ambition does not make every future feature a current requirement.

Review findings retain their severity and receive a separate delivery disposition. This tells implementation what blocks current acceptance, what is an iteration, and which deferred obligations actually belong on the ledger.

## Common questions

**Does personal dogfood mean accepting broken reasoning or retrieval?**

No. Required core outcomes and the evidence needed to establish them belong to the current proof. The mode reduces operational promises and evidence breadth; it does not turn a wrong result into a passing one.

**Will this make a minimal app difficult to grow later?**

The scale-ready reference preserves inexpensive boundaries with concrete reversal value. Each still has to pass OPTION for this application. The future deployment, tenancy or scale machinery waits for the relevant need and authority.

**Does every finding or missing test require another repair cycle?**

No. A current core/floor failure or essential missing evidence blocks readiness. Additional evidence and improvements are classified separately. Repeated claims without new evidence and oscillating fixes return to the disputed requirement before another repair cycle.

**Does installing it change existing project rules?**

No. Existing binding requirements and owner-approved qualifications remain authoritative. Reconciliation and promotion require their own authorized changes; a review alone edits neither rules nor code.

## It's working if

- Planning, implementation and independent reviewers cite the same current acceptance criteria.
- A real core defect blocks dogfood acceptance, while speculative hardening stays outside the current work.
- Small future boundaries have a concrete reason and one implementation.
- The agent stops when the agreed readiness evidence is established and its blockers are closed.
- Ordinary fixes reuse settled decisions instead of restarting the development process.
