## What it does

Maps consequential claims to evidence that could prove them wrong. One small shared map connects a requirement, its public entry point, relevant states, a decisive observable and the evidence actually obtained.

## When to reach for it

Type `/assurance-case`, or let the agent use it while changing or reviewing access, integrity, spending, lifecycle, provenance or audit behavior. It does not activate a general production checklist for ordinary edits.

## Falsifiable, not merely green

A test that passes even when the claimed behavior is wrong is not evidence for that claim. A red/green test or a small isolated fault probe demonstrates sensitivity. Mixed outcomes, alternate exits and stale/cache paths matter when they can change what the system reports.

## Common questions

**Do we need another governance file or a full mutation suite?**

No. Reuse the current plan, claim IDs and public seams. Select reachable risks rather than a blanket matrix. This is a focused method, not a new delivery gate.

**Can this replace executable safeguards?**

No. Prefer a typed shared seam and decisive tests where the contract earns them. Skill instructions alone cannot guarantee compliance or correctness.

## It's working if

- Each material claim has an observable counterexample and evidence status.
- Reported cost, status and provenance agree with what happened on the selected paths.
- The handoff names unvalidated claims without silently expanding live authority.
